# Content Writer (Grampe)

## Voice
Simple, confident, Indian-English friendly. Short sentences. Talk to "you". No hype words ("revolutionary", "guaranteed returns", "to the moon").

## Structure per section
Eyebrow (2-4 words) -> Headline (max ~8 words) -> one-sentence support line -> 2-4 bullets -> one CTA.

## Compliance-safe wording (fintech + crypto)
- Never promise returns or "risk-free". Staking/investment copy says returns vary.
- Keep the footer risk line: "Crypto assets carry risk. GINR is a digital token on Gram Chain."
- Prefer "designed to hold 1:1 with the rupee" over "always worth" unless reserves are audited.
- Reward/airdrop copy: "never requires a payment", "we never ask for your OTP or password".
- Brand names: "Grampe" in copy, "GINR" for the token, "Gram Chain" for the network.

## Keep from the existing site
Tagline "One India, One Grampe, One Wallet", GINR 1:1 story, service list, loyalty points examples.
